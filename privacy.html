Privacy Policy

GeoSidecar was designed so that the developer does not receive your photographs, location history, or identity.

Information GeoSidecar uses

The iPhone app uses precise location only when you explicitly start a Photo Day. Those locations form a GPX track used to geotag photographs. The Mac app reads capture metadata from folders and files you explicitly select.

Where information is stored

Photo Day sessions are stored locally on your iPhone. Completed tracks may synchronize through Apple's iCloud Drive into GeoSidecar's private container associated with your Apple Account. RAW files and generated XMP sidecars remain in the folder you select on your Mac.

Information the developer collects

The developer operates no server for GeoSidecar and does not receive or access your location tracks, photographs, RAW metadata, XMP sidecars, Apple Account information, or home regions. GeoSidecar contains no advertising, third-party analytics, cross-app tracking, or data brokerage SDKs.

Apple services

GeoSidecar uses Apple system services including Location Services, iCloud Drive, notifications, and MapKit. Apple's handling of information through those services is governed by Apple's privacy policies and your device and iCloud settings.

Your control

You may end location recording at any time, revoke location permission in system settings, export or delete completed tracks on iPhone, disable iCloud access, and delete generated XMP sidecars using Finder. Deleting a local track does not automatically delete a copy that was previously synchronized to iCloud.

Children

GeoSidecar is a professional photography utility and is not directed to children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated. Material changes will accompany an app update or updated App Store information.
