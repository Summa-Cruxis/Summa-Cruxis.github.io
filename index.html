Photography · iPhone + Mac + Lightroom Classic

Every photo, in the right place.

GeoSidecar records your location on iPhone, matches it to Hasselblad X2D RAW photographs on Mac, and sends approved GPS coordinates straight into your Lightroom Classic catalog. No XMP sidecars. No changes to your .3FR originals.

The workflow

Record on iPhone. Review on Mac. Confirm in Lightroom.

01 · RECORD

A Photo Day

Start a high-accuracy location track on iPhone before photographing. Optionally schedule a multi-day Photo Trip with daily track segments and camera-clock calibration.

02 · MATCH

See every location

Private iCloud sync brings tracks to Mac. Select RAWs already imported into Lightroom, preview matches on the map, and review accuracy, time zone and existing GPS.

03 · APPLY

GPS in your catalog

GeoSidecar Catalog Bridge writes approved coordinates into Lightroom Classic. The app distinguishes GPS confirmed as applied from updates still queued.

Built for photographers

Your originals and your edits stay yours.

RAW-SAFE

No sidecar clutter

The .3FR file is not modified, and GeoSidecar creates no XMP sidecars. It works with the GPS field in your active Lightroom Classic catalog.

PRECISE

Confidence before action

Exact, interpolated and uncertain matches are differentiated. Long gaps require review; existing GPS is protected unless you explicitly choose replacement.

PRIVATE

No account with us

iPhone tracks sync through your personal iCloud container. Manual GPX import is also available. Your location history is not sent to a Summa Cruxis server.

What you'll need

A clear, catalog-first workflow.

Use GeoSidecar on iPhone and Mac, a Hasselblad .3FR shoot, and Adobe Lightroom Classic on Mac with GeoSidecar Catalog Bridge enabled. Import the photographs into Lightroom before sending GPS. The Mac app provides a guided plug-in installation command. Installation instructions.

For traveling photographers, keep shooting in a Lightroom Travel catalog and later merge it into the master using File → Import from Another Catalog. Troubleshooting and common questions.
