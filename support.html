From Photo Day to Lightroom Classic

X2D GPS uses the capture time recorded by the camera to match each RAW photograph with the iPhone's location track.

1. Record on iPhone

  1. Open X2D GPS before the first photograph and tap Start Photo Day.
  2. Leave Photo Day running throughout the shoot. The app continues recording while the iPhone is locked.
  3. Optional: take one distinctive reference photograph, immediately return to X2D GPS, and tap I Just Took the Reference Photo.
  4. Tap End Photo Day after the shoot. Confirm that the track shows the green iCloud icon.

2. Review on Mac

  1. Open X2D GPS and wait for the completed Photo Day track to appear.
  2. If you created a calibration marker, select it and choose the exact reference RAW.
  3. Choose the folder containing the .3FR files and click Preview Matches.
  4. Review the confidence map and table. Green is exact, blue is interpolated, and orange requires review.

3. Create Lightroom sidecars

  1. Click Write XMP Sidecars.
  2. Confirm that each generated .xmp has the same filename stem as its .3FR partner.
  3. Import the RAW folder into Lightroom Classic. If the photographs were already imported, use Metadata → Read Metadata from File carefully.
Your RAW masters are safe. X2D GPS never writes into a .3FR file.

Common questions

My Mac does not see a completed track.

Confirm that both devices use the same Apple Account for iCloud Drive, that iCloud Drive is enabled for X2D GPS, and that the iPhone track shows the green synchronized icon. On Mac, click Refresh iCloud.

Do I need camera-clock calibration?

No. Use it when the camera clock may be early or late. One reference photo lets X2D GPS correct that drift across the entire shoot.

Why was a photograph blocked?

Low-confidence matches and existing GPS are blocked by default. Review the location first, then deliberately enable the relevant writing option if it is correct.
